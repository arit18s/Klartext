What to decide: did someone ask for one payment, transfer or deposit to be split into connected smaller parts, expressly so that a stated internal process (approval, check, reporting, limit) does not apply?
Harmless readings: several genuine separate invoices; contractual instalments such as rent or loan payments; splitting for a reason unrelated to any control; the advisor refuses.
Unclear: if parts may belong together and the reason for splitting them is not stated, use "undetermined" and name the missing fact (for example the original documents, due dates, the total, the real reason).
