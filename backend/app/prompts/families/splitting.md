Type: request family. The event is a request for connected partial payments, transfers or deposits that is expressly meant to keep a stated internal process (approval, check, reporting, limit) from applying. The advisor refusing does not undo the request.
Harmless readings: several genuine, separate invoices without any intended circumvention of a control; contractual instalments such as rent or loan payments; the speaker rejects or negates the circumvention themselves, or cites it as a training example.
Unclear: if the parts may belong together and the call does not establish a genuine reason for splitting them (original documents, due dates, total amount, real reason), use "undetermined" and name the missing facts. A speaker who does not know why the payments are split or whether they belong together does not make the split harmless.
