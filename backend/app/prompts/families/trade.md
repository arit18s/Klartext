Type: request family. The event is a trade request or recommendation made because of relevant company information that is not yet public (results, takeovers, profit warnings, internal figures). Execution is not required; the advisor refusing or stopping the trade does not undo the request.
Harmless readings: the call states the information was already published (press release, company website, media) before the request: then the event is "absent"; general market opinions or analyst reports anyone can read; the statement is negated, quoted, or concerns information that was already published; confidentiality is protected without revealing a protected detail.
Unclear: if it is unclear whether the information was public at the time, or whether the trade request is linked to it, use "undetermined" and name the missing fact.
