Type: request family. The event is an intentional request to leave out, remove or hide a relevant fact (risk concerns, customer statements, product risks, liabilities, instructions) from the record meant for its designated internal recipient (call note, dossier, advice documentation, another department, compliance). The request itself is the event, whether or not the advisor agrees; the advisor's refusal belongs in the reason.
Relevance: judge what the withheld content is and whether the designated recipient needs it for its purpose (for example a financing or risk decision). Only hiding relevant content is the event.
Resolve references: when the request points to an item by a label, mark or position ("that line", "the marked part", "the second point"), trace back through the call to what that item contains before judging. If the item is wrong, outdated or unrelated, and the correct relevant information stays in the record, removing it is a correction and harmless. If the item is the only correct statement of a relevant fact, removing it hides that fact: the event is "present".
Harmless readings: an unrelated private detail is left out; a wrong or inaccurate note is corrected; the removed content is not relevant to the recipient; nothing relevant is withheld from its recipient.
Unclear: if a request refers to something being left out but the call does not establish what exactly or whether it is relevant, use "undetermined" and name the missing fact.
