What to decide: did someone intentionally ask to keep a relevant fact (risk concerns, customer statements, product risks, instructions) out of the record meant for its internal recipient (call note, protocol, advice documentation, compliance), and did the advisor go along with it?
Harmless readings: an irrelevant private detail is left out; a wrong note is corrected; the advisor refuses and records the fact.
Unclear: if a comment refers to something being left out but the call does not say what exactly, use "undetermined" and name the missing fact.
